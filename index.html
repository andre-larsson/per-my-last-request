The rulebook

You write the orders. The hero makes the decisions. Each turn the hero picks one of the offered actions, guided only by your standing orders. Change the orders, run again, compare.

Assignments

  • 01 · First instructions: earn the practice rat’s stamp and submit at camp within 12 turns.
  • 02 · Fair warning: earn the rat, boar and Quickfoot stamps and submit at camp within 16 turns.
  • 03 · Prize money: the circuit’s second half. Earn Quickfoot’s stamp, beat the champion and submit at camp within 30 turns.
  • 04 · The full circuit: earn three stamps, beat the champion and submit at camp within 32 turns.
  • Every action takes one turn. Model loading and thinking take none.
  • Passing camp unfinished starts a new lap: opponents gain 3 HP and 1 damage.

Fighting

  • Quick strike: 6 damage, restores 1 stamina.
  • Heavy strike: 12 damage, costs 2 stamina.
  • Armor blocks its value from every strike. Agile enemies take only 3 from a heavy strike.
  • A surviving enemy hits back after each strike or potion.
  • Retreat: lose 2 HP, forfeit the reward.

Reading the opponent from 02

  • Every opponent repeats a routine and announces its move before the hero acts.
  • Strike: an ordinary hit. Smash: a big hit.
  • Bracing: no hit this turn, and strikes deal half. Winded: no hit, and an agile enemy cannot dodge.
  • Guard: deal nothing, block up to 8 damage, restore 1 stamina.
  • Guarding a smash counters it: the enemy takes a heavy strike’s damage at once.

Staying alive

  • The hero starts with 40 HP, 4 stamina and 2 potions.
  • Potion: restores up to 18 HP and takes a turn.
  • Health is flagged LOW at 18 HP or less. The hero is told this.
  • Field medic: 12 HP, free, once per trial.
  • Bench: full stamina and 6 HP, once per lap. Walking restores 1 stamina.

Coins and gear

  • The prize purse holds 6 coins. Wins pay 2, the optional prize bruiser 6, the champion 8.
  • Armorer: a sharper sword (+2 damage on every strike) or a buckler (guard blocks 6 more) for 8 coins each; a potion for 6. Each purchase takes a turn.

Commendations full circuit, optional

  • Ahead of schedule: submit by turn 26.
  • No expense claims: win without drinking a potion.
  • Overtime: beat the optional prize bruiser too.
  • By the book: never take a smash without guarding.

Manual sandbox lets you click the actions yourself to learn the rules. It does not read your orders.
